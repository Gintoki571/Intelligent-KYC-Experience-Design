Challenge 4: Intelligent KYC Experience - Smart Form Assistance and Support Ticket Reduction
Challenge Overview
Digital onboarding through KYC verification is often slowed down by complex form filling, frequent data entry errors, and lack of clarity around verification status. These issues lead to repeated submission attempts, user frustration, incomplete onboarding, and a high volume of customer support inquiries.
Participants are challenged to design an intelligent KYC experience that simplifies data entry, and provides transparent status communication, ensuring a smoother onboarding journey for users.
Challenge Objective
Design a smart KYC onboarding solution that:
Reduces errors during data entry
Improves transparency of KYC progress and verification outcomes
Reduces KYC-related support inquiries
Problem Scope
The solution should focus on improving the user experience and operational efficiency of the KYC journey, specifically covering:
KYC form data entry assistance
Clear communication of KYC progress and outcomes
The challenge does not require redesigning regulatory compliance rules, but rather improving how users interact with the system.
Key Issues
Users frequently make mistakes while entering KYC data leading to resubmissions.
Prolonged and complex form filling process increases abandonment.
Customer support receives a high volume of inquiries regarding KYC status despite status visibility.
Users often do not understand the verification stage, rejection reasons, or expected processing time.
Guiding Questions
Participants may consider the following questions while designing their solutions:
How can the system detect form errors before submission?
How can KYC forms be simplified to reduce user effort?
How can document data be automatically captured to minimize manual entry?
How can the KYC progress be communicated clearly to users?
How can the system reduce KYC-related support queries?
What Teams Should Design and Build
Participants should design a solution that enhances the KYC journey through intelligent assistance and transparency. The system may include:
Smart KYC form interface with contextual assistance
Automated data capture from uploaded identity documents
Real-time input validation and error detection
Clear KYC progress indicators and verification stage explanations
Notifications and guidance when additional actions are required
Expected Impact
Successful solutions should aim to achieve:
Faster KYC onboarding completion
Higher first-time submission success rate
Reduced KYC abandonment rate
Reduced KYC-related customer support tickets
Improved user understanding of verification progress
